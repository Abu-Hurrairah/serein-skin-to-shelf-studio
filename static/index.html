ABOUT THE STUDIO

Designed for better decisions.

Serein is a skin-to-shelf decision studio. Real scans use Perfect Corp's YouCam Skin Analysis v2.1 and produce cosmetic guidance, a prioritised routine, and fewer unnecessary purchases.

Privacy: the app doesn't store selfie files on its server. For live analysis, your photo is transmitted to YouCam with consent. Journal scores and notes stay in your browser's local storage.

An illustrative sample is available without an API key and is always labelled. Scores are appearance indicators, not medical measurements.
